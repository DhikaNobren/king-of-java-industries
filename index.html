Our Story

A workshop built around the grain of Javanese timber.

King of Java Furniture Industries began as a small joinery workshop in West Java and has grown into a full-scale manufacturing house serving residential, hospitality and export clients across the globe — without losing the hand-finished character that started it all.

Mission

Craft enduring furniture that honours the wood it comes from.

Vision

Be Indonesia's most trusted export furniture partner.

Integrity

Honest material, honest pricing, honest timelines.

Craft

Precision joinery over shortcuts, every time.

2005
Workshop founded

Started as a small solid-wood joinery workshop serving local clients in West Java.

2012
First export orders

Expanded into container-scale export production for hospitality buyers abroad.

2018
OEM division launched

Began white-label manufacturing for international furniture and hospitality brands.

2026
Nationwide production scale

Operating a full manufacturing facility with in-house QC and export logistics.

Manufacturing Process

From log to living room, in nine deliberate steps.

01
Material Selection

Grading timber for grain and structural quality.

02

03

04

05
